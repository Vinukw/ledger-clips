Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Ledger Clips ("the app") is a personal tool that uploads videos to its owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What the app accesses

  • Basic profile information of the signed-in account (such as display name and account ID), used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos to that account, used only to upload videos the owner chooses.

Storage

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to make the uploads described above. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. The app has no servers and no database of users.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok or Google account settings ("Manage app permissions"). Deleting the local token files removes all stored access.
